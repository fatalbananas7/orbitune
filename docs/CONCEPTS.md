# Concept Generation

1. **Orbitune** — *You are a singing comet.* Orbit glowing "bell-stars"; release to slingshot tangentially and get caught by the next star's gravity. Every catch plays a note; chaining catches builds melodies, same-colour catches form chords. Controls: release to launch, hold = slow-time aim with trajectory preview, swipe = reverse orbit. Addictiveness: very high — 1-input skill timing, "perfect" catches, rising music as reward, sector milestones.
2. **Ink Tide** — paint shorelines with a finger to steer a rising ink flood around villages. Controls: drag. Addictive: medium; physics fluid costly on phones.
3. **Lantern Courier** — a moth swaps between light and shadow planes by two-thumb taps while delivering lanterns. Addictive: medium; reads like a runner.
4. **Clockwork Garden** — rotate gear-rings so seeds fall into sockets; part puzzle, part timing. Addictive: medium-high but puzzle content is expensive.
5. **Echo Burglar** — sonar taps reveal the room for a moment; sneak past guards. Addictive: medium; slow sessions.

**Pick: Orbitune.** Strongest skill-expression per input, natural 30–180 s sessions (fuel burns down), procedural infinite content, and a unique hook — your play literally composes the soundtrack (catch notes follow a pentatonic scale, combos layer the music). Slingshot-orbit + rhythm/chord scoring + fuel economy is not a known genre template.
